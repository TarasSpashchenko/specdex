## Answer A

- server-credential requests by an unlocked device (FR-88);
- the US referral count (FR-104);
- store traffic (Google Play purchases and age signal).

Each exception follows its own authorization and retention rules, and child speech is never sent in basic mode. A parent's request made offline is sent only once a connection returns. Counters and crash reports need the parent's opt-in, which is marked `[ASSUMPTION: CQ-21]`, so that part is provisional. Credential requests need the unlock and active consent.
