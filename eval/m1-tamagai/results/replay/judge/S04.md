- **When ears return:** Offline lasts until the next visit with a connection. That is flagged `[ASSUMPTION: not mid-visit]`, so it is provisional. Heat lasts until the device cools.
- **Parent area text:** Offline shows "The device is offline". Heat shows "The nap, then resting ears until the device cools". Heat therefore starts with a nap before the resting ears begin.
