## Answer C

No FR gives a parent that ability. The spec rules it out, so you won't find an ID to attach a story to.

- The PRD's parent-area requirement, FR-65 "What grown-ups see", lists what a parent can see. That is play time, visits, word cards, keepsakes, care actions and the child's flagged lines. It also says the parent area "never shows recordings, transcripts, anything the child said, mood, or helpline events."
- The addendum records a decision to drop a "parent list of Pip's recent lines" (the alternative was keeping the last 20 lines). The stated reason is that it "restores 'never transcripts' and removes the trace risk." It is also listed among the exclusions (item 10).
- FR-26 "Pip's lines" is about what Pip may say and how it is captioned. It is not a parent-facing view.
- If your story is about parent visibility, FR-65 is the closest FR. The child's flagged lines are the only line-level content a parent sees. A transcript story would need a PRD change first.
