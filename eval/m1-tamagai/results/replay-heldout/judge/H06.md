# Task H06

**Question:** Where may we process recordings collected from children trying the app, and can we send them to our cloud testing setup?

**Gold answer:** Research-panel voices must stay on the developer machine and the LAN host. They cannot be sent to cloud staging or production.

**Gold evidence (current record):**
- _bmad-output/planning-artifacts/architecture/architecture-tamagai-2026-10-03/ARCHITECTURE-OVERVIEW.md:135-135 — The release-gate list item restricts research-panel voices to the developer machine and LAN host.

**Labeller notes:** Discovery of the research-audio location restriction, phrased without research-panel or LAN terminology.

## Answer A

Recordings from children trying the app are research-panel clips (the separately consented group of children). The specs let you process them only for the evaluation suite. They must stay in encrypted, access-listed storage that you control, and the specs say "no transfer". They're kept until the end of the program, or 24 months at most. The data map says they never leave "our encrypted store". Architecture puts the evaluation harness with the encrypted panel clips on the developer machine. It sends audio to a LAN GPU host that "keeps nothing".

You can't send them to cloud staging. The EU staging environment is "actor/synthetic audio only", and that is where load tests run. I found no spec that approves a third-party cloud testing service for panel audio. "No transfer" and counsel question CQ-28 (consent and retention; whether evaluation counts as "improving AI") leave that unresolved, so you would need a change to the specs plus counsel sign-off. CR-10 must be in place before any panel recording. Crisis phrases are never recorded from the panel.

## Answer B

Children’s test recordings may be processed only on the developer machine or the LAN host through an SSH tunnel; the LAN host must be registered under CR-10 and pass the AD-24 settings check. **No, they cannot be sent to cloud testing/staging**, which accepts only actor and synthetic audio. These recordings require separate written parental consent for evaluation only, encrypted access-listed storage, separation from player data, and deletion at program end or within 24 months, whichever comes first.

## Answer C

Children’s research-panel recordings may be processed only on the developer machine or the LAN host, reached through an SSH tunnel. The LAN host must be named in the CR-10 register, and its model-server settings must pass a preflight check before receiving clips. **No, they cannot go to cloud staging**; staging uses actor or synthetic audio, including all crisis audio. Before recording, CR-10 requires separate written parental consent for evaluation only, encrypted access-listed storage, withdrawal provisions, consent/deletion records, separation from player data, and retention until program end or at most 24 months.

## Answer D

Recordings from the research panel (children whose voices are used for evaluation) may be processed only on the developer machine and on the LAN host, and the LAN host is reached through an SSH tunnel only. They may not go to staging or to any cloud testing setup. Staging and the LAN host are for actor and synthetic audio only. The product requirements (PRD) add that panel clips live in our encrypted, access-listed store, with no transfer. Retention is the end of the program or 24 months at most. They are never mixed with player data, and parental consent covers evaluation only. The LAN host must also be named in the CR-10 register, and a preflight check must pass before any clip is sent to it.

I found no passage that names a cloud testing service. The data-routing table simply doesn't list one as an allowed destination, so the answer is no unless the product owner and counsel change CR-10 and AD-29.
