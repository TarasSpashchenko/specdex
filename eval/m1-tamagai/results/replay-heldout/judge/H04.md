## Answer B

With the phone's "Remove animations" setting on, the app follows it, and there is no separate parent setting for this in v1. Pip plays calm variants. They keep the same timing and cues, with squash halved, feet no more than 5% off the ground, and no flips.

Big transitions change in two ways:
- Any element that travels more than a third of the screen, or scales more than 10%, becomes a crossfade of 150 ms or less. This covers the word-card fly-in, drawer and tray rise, wish pop, dream entry and diary page turn.
- Narrator frames and the helpline takeover crossfade, and the time-of-day swap becomes a 150 ms crossfade.

The signal that the phone is hearing the child (the ear glow) still moves. It keeps its 1.6 s pulse, matching the asset pack's calm variant. The hint ring and dream cloud hold still. Calm audio also follows the same setting for now.
