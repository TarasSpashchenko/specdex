## Answer A

No, you may not use children from your research panel (the tryouts) to record crisis phrases such as self-harm statements. The spec treats asking a child to record these phrases as a safeguarding harm. Crisis test audio must come only from paid adult actors, child-voice actors, or synthetic child voices. The research panel still supplies other evaluation audio, but never crisis audio. The rule is recorded as CR-10 and is an Evidence-labelled safeguarding decision. The CR-1 child-safety experts sign off the crisis test set. For the family-beta suite, the product owner signs off a provisional text set instead, with actors' audio.

The "tryouts" are my reading of the research panel (CR-10). If you meant playtests (NFR-17), those aren't covered by this rule, and I didn't check them for crisis content.
